# Task 5 - Sales Data Analysis
**Dataset:** Superstore Sales Dataset
**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn

## Problem Statement
Analyze business performance through sales trends, 
top products, and profit analysis.

## Analysis Performed
- Monthly revenue trend analysis
- Top 10 best selling products
- Sales vs Profit by category
- Top 10 states by sales
- Sub-category profit & loss analysis

## Key Insights
- Technology is the most profitable category
- Furniture has high sales but low profit margin
- Tables and Bookcases are loss-making sub-categories
- California is the top state by sales
